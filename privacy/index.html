Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Securelink Defence International Limited ("Securelink Defence", "we", "us"), a Primers Group company based in Abuja, Nigeria, builds emergency response technology. This policy explains what we collect through this website and the SecureLink platform, why, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Contact details you send us, such as your name, email address, organisation and message when you request a briefing or write to us.
  • Emergency data inside SecureLink, when you use the platform: your account details, the location of your device at the moment an alert is raised, the type of incident, and the incident record as it moves to the responders who handle it.
  • Technical data our hosting provider records automatically, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, used to keep the service secure and working.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To route an emergency to the responders and agencies responsible for it, and to keep an auditable record of what happened.
  • To answer your enquiries and arrange briefings.
  • To secure, maintain and improve our services, and to meet our legal obligations.

Who we share it with

During an incident, we share the information needed to respond with the responders, agencies and contacts the alert is routed to. We use service providers for hosting, messaging and infrastructure, who process data only on our instructions. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose information where the law requires it or to protect someone's life or safety.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and follow up. Incident records are kept for as long as needed for response, accountability and legal requirements, then deleted or anonymised.

Security

We protect data with encryption in transit, access controls and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure, so we also limit what we collect to what a response needs.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, or withdraw consent where we rely on it. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. To exercise any right, email primerscorperation@gmail.com.

Not an emergency service

Securelink Defence provides technology and coordination infrastructure. It does not replace official emergency numbers. In an emergency, call 112.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
